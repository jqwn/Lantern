Lantern — early preview for Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 13 or later

Installation
1. Drag Lantern.app to Applications. Quit an older copy first when playback can be interrupted.
2. Eject the Lantern disk image, then open Lantern from Applications.
3. Allow local-network access and incoming connections if macOS asks.

Gatekeeper warning
This app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized by Apple.
macOS may block the first launch. Only if you trust this download, open
System Settings > Privacy & Security after trying to launch, choose Open Anyway,
and confirm. Managed Macs may not allow this exception. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
An update may require approval again.

Apple's guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-au/102445

FFmpeg and ffprobe are NOT bundled. Ordinary sharing and existing sidecar
subtitles work without them; inspecting and preparing embedded text subtitles
requires a separate FFmpeg installation.

Prepare English for Library extracts full English subtitles first, then downloads
confident file-hash matches from OpenSubtitles if needed. Searches send a video
fingerprint, never the video or its filename. Anonymous downloads are limited
to 5 per day per IP. Uncertain matches are left unresolved; originals are unchanged.

Use only on a trusted home network. Sharing has no password or encryption.
Lantern streams the original files unchanged; TV codec support still applies.

Source and help: https://github.com/jqwn/Lantern
